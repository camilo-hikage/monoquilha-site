The concept

From breakfast
to the late-night bar

monoquilha was born from a cosmopolitan brunch with local influence: regional suppliers and produce meet the best of original cuisine, right across from the Ubatuba waterfront.

By day, the house opens early for breakfast and brunch. By night, it turns into a latasian — Latin-Asian — menu served alongside a craft cocktail bar, in a cozy atmosphere with good music.
